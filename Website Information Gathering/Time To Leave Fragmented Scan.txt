By this we can check time to send a fragmented packet from sender to target.Basically we can check the time interval  between two packets.

cmd  --> ping target.com -i 1
    -i means the time interval between two packets ot TTL (Time Ti Live)

Here we have to manually search the minimum value of i .. Typically it is 7

ping target.com -i 7


cmd --> ping target.com -i 7 -n 10
    -n means the number of packets to be sent
    Here total 10 packets will be sent with a time interval of 7 sec between two packets.
